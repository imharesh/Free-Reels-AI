Welcome to the Free Reels AI documentation. Free Reels AI is an open-source creator studio for producing short-form videos with the providers you choose.

The software is free and self-hosted. Some connected image, video, voice, or hosted-model providers may charge for usage; you can use local models, local voice, bundled music, and stock media to reduce those costs.
